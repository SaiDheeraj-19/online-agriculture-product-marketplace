How it works

Three steps from field to front door.

  1. 01

    Browse what's listed

    Every listing shows the farmer who grew it, the price they set and how much stock is left.

  2. 02

    Order from the farm

    Add items to your basket and check out with a delivery address. Stock is reserved when you order.

  3. 03

    Follow it to your door

    Farmers confirm and ship your order. You see each status change on your orders page.
