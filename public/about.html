About AgriMart

A shorter road between the farm and the kitchen.

Most produce passes through several hands before it reaches you, and each one adds to the price. AgriMart is an online marketplace where farmers sell directly to customers. The farmer sets the price and the customer sees exactly who they're buying from.

01

Farmers set the price

No one between the farmer and the buyer changes what a product costs.

02

Stock you can trust

Quantities come from the farmer's own listing and drop as soon as an order is placed.

03

Orders you can follow

Each order moves through pending, confirmed, shipped and delivered, and both sides see every step.

The project

Built as a full-stack marketplace.

AgriMart was designed and built by R Sai Dheeraj as an online agriculture product marketplace. It runs on Express.js with a MySQL database, and the front end is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

  • Separate customer and farmer accounts
  • Product listings with live stock
  • Basket, checkout and order tracking
