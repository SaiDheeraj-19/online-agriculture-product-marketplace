Getting started

From sign-up to first order.

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Choose "Farmer" when you register. You only need a name, email and password.

  2. 02

    Add your produce

    Give each product a name, category, price and stock count. Add a short description so buyers know what they're getting.

  3. 03

    Fulfil orders

    New orders appear on your dashboard. Move each one from pending to confirmed, shipped and delivered.
